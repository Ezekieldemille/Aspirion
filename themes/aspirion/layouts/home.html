AN INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Aspirion.org!

We are so glad you are here!

This website is to share with you my philosophy. The thoughts shared here represent the synthesis of many areas of study I have engaged with across my life. I do not claim that it is universal, unquestionable, much less complete.

But, as a young man, this website would have been a resource I needed. I hope it could have helped me avoid many things I now regret having done or believed.

So, believing in the Gandhian ethic of being the change you wish to see, I have stuck out my neck to share with you my personal belief system, as well as publishing significant portions of my own progress in following through with these principles.

Please! Read below. Take what you find valuable, leave what you don't, and Become.

May you find Truth.
May you Struggle.
May you Become.

And may this be a place to aid you in those pursuits.

As we breathe.

GETTING TO KNOW ASPIRION

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aspirion?

Aspirion is a nonsupernatural religion that promotes the use of religion as a social and spiritual technology to promote self-development, and by extension, community development.

Click on the link below to learn more about Aspirion's philosophy, and then judge for yourself whether or not it sounds like something you would like to incorporate into your life.

What do you call someone who follows Aspirion?
