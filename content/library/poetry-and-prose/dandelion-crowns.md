As I walk along I'm looking down  
I look for dandelions golden crowns  
And for the worker bees that call the fields here home

Not for fear that they'll sting me  
But that my foot might kill a bee  
And break that delicate soul

I know they work here where the sun resounds  
Collecting sugar with low sounds  
That hum a background melody

And I would not want to be a cloud  
Not want to cast a shroud  
And stop their work half done

So I watch my way as I walk  
And speak lowly as I talk  
So I can hear their warning hum

And as the day begins to dim  
I hang a lantern on the limb  
To light their way back home

And I hope that God treats me  
How in Mercy I've treated bees  
And leaves a door open to his feast

For I know I've not him felt  
But neither have I cast stones to pelt  
At the house I'm told he's built for me

For though an Atheist am I  
Still I wander and I cry  
And miss the certainty I knew

For the warmth of God, though never felt  
Is something I've always felt has dwelt  
Just beyond my reach

So I'll keep watching for a bee  
And hope that God will be there for me  
If indeed he waits beyond

And if no God is to be found  
I will play god upon this ground  
And be who I was taught he would be

I'll be who I was taught he would be  
Be who I needed him to be
